Content areas (course schedule)
  • Amino acids and peptides: classification, properties, acid–base behaviour and titration curves
  • Higher-order protein structure; chaperones; prion diseases and Alzheimer’s disease
  • Protein determination: Edman and Sanger reactions, electrophoresis, chromatography, spectrometry
  • Hemoglobin and the chemistry of respiration: T and R states, oxygen dissociation, the Bohr and Haldane effects, the chloride (Hamburger) shift
  • Hemoglobinopathies: the thalassemias and sickle cell anemia
  • Enzymes: classification, kinetics, Michaelis–Menten, inhibitors and Lineweaver–Burk plots, diagnostic enzymes and markers of myocardial infarction
  • Collagen: synthesis and structure; Ehlers–Danlos and Alport syndromes, epidermolysis bullosa, osteogenesis imperfecta, scurvy
High-yield emphasis

Essential amino acids; isoelectric point; the bonds that hold each level of structure; T and R states, 2,3-BPG and the Bohr effect; the HbS mutation; Km and Vmax under competitive and noncompetitive inhibition; troponin and CK-MB; vitamin C in collagen hydroxylation; the collagen type behind each collagen disorder
